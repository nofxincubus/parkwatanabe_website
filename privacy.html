Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Park Watanabe LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is a core principle. We design our software — including CloudAudio and WishWhale — to minimize data collection and respect your personal boundary.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications are architected to store data locally on your device or via your private cloud storage (such as Apple iCloud / Google Drive):

  • Account & Content Data: Playlists, preferences, wishlists, and user configurations are synced directly using your own authenticated cloud accounts. We do not maintain central databases of your personal items.
  • Diagnostics: With your opt-in consent, anonymous crash logs or performance telemetry may be collected via platform stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) to diagnose bugs.

2. How We Use Information

Any collected data is solely used to deliver, maintain, and improve the features within our applications. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

3. Third-Party Services

Certain features may link or stream from third-party services (e.g., streaming providers or web scrapers for wishlist links). Those external services operate under their own independent privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at privacy@parkwatanabe.com.
